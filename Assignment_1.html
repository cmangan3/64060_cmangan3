Quantitative Variable Plot

The following histogram displays the distribution of penguin body mass.

hist(penguins$body_mass_g,
     main = "Distribution of Penguin Body Mass",
     xlab = "Body Mass (grams)")

Scatterplot

The following scatterplot examines the relationship between flipper length and body mass.

plot(penguins$flipper_length_mm,
     penguins$body_mass_g,
     main = "Flipper Length vs. Body Mass",
     xlab = "Flipper Length (mm)",
     ylab = "Body Mass (g)")
